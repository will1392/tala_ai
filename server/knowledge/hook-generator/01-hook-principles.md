# Core Hook Writing Principles

## What Makes a Hook Effective?

A great hook achieves these goals in the first 5-20 words:
1. **Stops the scroll** - Creates a pattern interrupt
2. **Sparks curiosity** - Opens a knowledge gap
3. **Promises value** - Hints at the outcome
4. **Matches awareness** - Speaks to where the prospect is mentally
5. **Builds desire** - Makes them want to read more

## The 5-Second Test

Your hook must pass this test: If someone gives you 5 seconds, will they:
- Understand what you're offering?
- Feel compelled to learn more?
- Believe it's relevant to them?

If the answer is no to any of these, revise the hook.

## Core Principles

### 1. Specificity Beats Generality

❌ **Generic**: "Improve your travel business"  
✅ **Specific**: "Add $47K in bookings this quarter using this 3-email sequence"

**Why**: Specific numbers and timeframes create believability and urgency.

### 2. Lead with Value, Not Features

❌ **Feature-focused**: "Our new booking platform has 50+ features"  
✅ **Value-focused**: "Cut your itinerary planning time from 6 hours to 45 minutes"

**Why**: People care about outcomes, not capabilities.

### 3. Create Curiosity Gaps

❌ **Complete**: "Here's how to book more luxury clients"  
✅ **Gap**: "The counterintuitive approach that books 3x more luxury clients"

**Why**: The brain craves closure on open loops.

### 4. Use the Power of "You"

❌ **Third person**: "Travelers love personalized itineraries"  
✅ **Direct**: "Your clients will thank you for this itinerary hack"

**Why**: Direct address creates personal connection.

### 5. Front-Load the Benefit

❌ **Buried**: "Using our proprietary system, which we developed over 5 years, you can save time"  
✅ **Front-loaded**: "Save 6 hours per booking with this proven system"

**Why**: Attention spans are 3 seconds. Get to the point.

### 6. Match the Prospect's Mental State

For someone who doesn't know they have a problem:
- ❌ "Buy our CRM software"
- ✅ "Are you still tracking clients in spreadsheets?"

For someone ready to buy:
- ❌ "You might have a client tracking problem"
- ✅ "Get lifetime access to our CRM for 40% off (ends Friday)"

### 7. Use Power Words Strategically

**Curiosity**: secret, hidden, revealed, truth, exposed, confessions
**Urgency**: now, today, deadline, limited, closing, final
**Value**: proven, guaranteed, tested, certified, validated
**Transformation**: from X to Y, before/after, breakthrough

### 8. Avoid These Hook Killers

❌ **Salesy language**: "Buy now!" "Limited time offer!" "Act today!"  
❌ **Vague promises**: "Success awaits" "Transform your business"  
❌ **Overused phrases**: "Game-changer" "One weird trick" "Gurus don't want you to know"  
❌ **Hype without substance**: "Make millions overnight!"

### 9. The Pattern Interrupt Formula

To stop the scroll, break expectations:

**Formula**: [Unexpected claim] + [Specific outcome] + [For specific person]

Examples:
- "Stop networking. Here's how 3 cold emails landed me $140K in contracts"
- "I quit social media and doubled my bookings. Here's the system"
- "Fire 80% of your marketing tactics. Keep these 3"

### 10. Test Against the Awareness Ladder

Before finalizing a hook, ask:

- **Unaware**: Does it spark curiosity without requiring knowledge?
- **Problem Aware**: Does it agitate the pain they feel?
- **Solution Aware**: Does it position your solution as the answer?
- **Product Aware**: Does it differentiate you from alternatives?
- **Most Aware**: Does it drive immediate action?

## Advanced Techniques

### The "Negative Angle"

People are 2-3x more motivated to avoid loss than gain pleasure.

❌ **Positive**: "How to save time on client emails"  
✅ **Negative**: "Stop wasting 10 hours a week on client emails"

### The "Qualifier Hook"

Self-selection increases engagement.

"Only for travel advisors billing $500K+ per year:"
"If you're still manually creating itineraries, read this:"

### The "Contrarian Take"

Challenge conventional wisdom.

"Forget Instagram. This 'boring' platform generated $280K in bookings"
"Why I stopped offering custom itineraries (and doubled revenue)"

### The "Story Snippet"

Start with a moment of transformation.

"Three months ago, I was crying in my home office, $40K in debt.  
Today I closed a $95K group booking. Here's the exact email I sent..."

## Hook Quality Checklist

Before shipping a hook, verify:

- [ ] Under 20 words (ideal: 8-15)
- [ ] No banned phrases or salesy CTAs
- [ ] Specific numbers or timeframes included
- [ ] Passes the 5-second test
- [ ] Matches the target awareness level
- [ ] Creates curiosity or urgency
- [ ] Front-loads the main benefit
- [ ] Uses "you" language (when appropriate)
- [ ] Tested against alternatives

## Common Mistakes

1. **Too long** - If you need 30 words to hook, it's not a hook
2. **Too vague** - "Better results" means nothing
3. **Wrong awareness** - Selling features to someone who doesn't know they have a problem
4. **No payoff** - Creating curiosity with no intention to deliver
5. **Copying competitors** - Your hook needs to differentiate

## Resources for Improvement

- Study Eugene Schwartz's "Breakthrough Advertising" (awareness levels)
- Analyze top-performing subject lines in your industry
- A/B test every hook (aim for 2-5% lift)
- Keep a swipe file of hooks that make YOU want to click
