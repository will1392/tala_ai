# PPC Advertising Best Practices

Pay-per-click advertising is one of the most effective ways to drive targeted traffic to your website.

## Keyword Selection

- Focus on commercial intent keywords
- Use negative keywords to reduce waste
- Start with exact match, then expand
- Monitor search terms report weekly

## Ad Copy Templates

**Search Ad Template 1: Problem/Solution**
Headline 1: {{Problem}} Solved
Headline 2: {{Solution}} - {{Benefit}}
Description: {{Detailed_benefit}}. {{Social_proof}}. {{CTA}}

**Search Ad Template 2: Benefit-Focused**
Headline 1: {{Primary_Benefit}}
Headline 2: {{Secondary_Benefit}} | {{Brand}}
Description: {{Feature_list}}. {{Urgency}}. {{CTA}}

## Bidding Strategies

1. **Manual CPC**: Best for new campaigns with limited data
2. **Target CPA**: Once you have 30+ conversions
3. **Target ROAS**: For e-commerce with varied product values
4. **Maximize Conversions**: With a set budget cap

## Landing Page Checklist

- [ ] Message match with ad copy
- [ ] Single clear CTA above fold

- [ ] Mobile-responsive design
