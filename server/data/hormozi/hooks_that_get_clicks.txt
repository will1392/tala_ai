- Statement hooks declare a sharp benefit; question hooks earn curiosity; commands push decisive action; conditionals frame "if this, then that"; story seeds tease unfinished drama.
- Most aware prospects want proof you can deliver again; product aware prospects want your differentiator; solution aware wants clarity; problem aware needs pain agitated; unaware needs curiosity or intrigue.
- Banned phrases: cut jargon like "unlock your potential" and mushy verbs that weaken the promise.
- Active voice + human rhythm: read aloud before shipping; anything clunky gets cut.
- No multi-idea lines. If you need a conjunction, write a new hook.
